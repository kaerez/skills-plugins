## No Tracking or Analytics

This Repository and its published content do not include any tracking pixels,
analytics scripts, telemetry agents, or crash reporters added by us.
Repository documentation (README and other Markdown files) does make use of
third-party badge rendering via [shields.io](https://shields.io). When these
badges are rendered, your browser makes a request to shields.io servers; this
is governed by [shields.io's own privacy policy](https://shields.io/privacy).
Additionally, GitHub, as the hosting platform for this Repository, may
independently collect usage and analytics data in accordance with
[GitHub's Privacy Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children's Privacy

The skills and plugins in this Repository are not directed at children under
the age of 13. No data is knowingly collected from minors.
